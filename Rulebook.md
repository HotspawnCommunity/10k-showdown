# Hotspawn 10k Showdown | Official Tournament Rulebook

---

## 1.0 Definitions & Core Framework

- **Hotspawn Community:** The community and tournament branch of Hotspawn.
- **10K Showdown ("The Tournament"):** The entire tournament scope, from Open Qualifiers to Group Stages to LAN Finals.
- **Participants:** Teams and players who participate in Hotspawn events.
- **Tournament Officials:** Referees and other officials involved in operating Hotspawn events.
- **Team Core:** A team’s core consists of three (3) players out of the five (5) registered players at a minimum.
- **Publisher / Valve:** Valve Corporation, the publisher of Counter-Strike 2 (CS2).
- **Valve Regional Standings (VRS):** The official system for ranking performance published by Valve.
- **Valve Tournament Operating Requirements (TOR):** Official requirements to operate Tier 1 or Tier 2 events created by Valve.
- **LAN Finals:** The final stage of the competition hosted offline in Sheffield, United Kingdom.

### 3.3 Direct VRS Invitations & Pass-Down Logic

- Four (4) teams receive direct invitations to the Group Stage.
- Invitations utilise the Valve Regional Standings for Europe as of August 6th, 2026.
- Invitations start from 13th place in the Europe VRS standings, provided the team is not ranked in the Top 12 globally.
- If a team is Top 12 globally, the invite passes to the next eligible seed.
- Teams have 72 hours to accept an invite, or it is sent to the next seed down.
- If eligible VRS teams are lower than available slots, Hotspawn reserves the right to reallocate unfilled slots to Open Qualifiers.

## 4.0 Eligibility & Roster Rules

### 4.1 Player & Location Requirements

- **Physical Location & Region:** Permanent European residency is not required; however, all participating players must be physically located and playing from within the [**Valve Europe (EU) Region**](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/counter-strike_regional_standings/blob/main/model/util/region.js) for every match of the tournament. Regional boundaries strictly adhere to Valve's official Regional Standings definitions.
- **Bootcamps & Traveling Rosters:** International teams or players visiting from other regions (e.g., participating from an official European bootcamp or temporary facility) are fully eligible to compete, provided all active players are physically playing from within the defined Valve Europe region at the time of their matches.
- **Age Restriction:** All players must be at least 16 years of age by the start of the tournament.
- **FACEIT Account Status:** Players must adhere to FACEIT site rules and cannot have an active login or game ban. Attempting to bypass a ban using alt accounts will result in immediate team disqualification.
- **Anti-Cheat Requirements:** All players must install, update, and run the designated Anti-Cheat software (e.g., FACEIT Anti-Cheat / Akros Anti-Cheat) to maintain match eligibility.

### 4.2 Open Qualifier Registration & Roster Lock (Non-VRS Teams)

This rule applies specifically to non-VRS ranked teams entering the tournament via the Open Qualifiers.

- **Primary Team Lineup:** Upon signing up for an Open Qualifier, the five (5) players selected as the starting lineup on the registration roster will be officially designated as the team's **Primary Lineup** should the team qualify for the Online Group Stage.
- **Qualifier Substitutes & Stand-ins:** If a team requires a stand-in during an Open Qualifier, that stand-in must be officially registered as one of the team’s two (2) allowed substitutes and must **not** be listed among the five primary starting players.
- **Core Roster Retention:** In accordance with the 3/5 Core Roster Rule, at least three (3) of the five primary starting players from the qualified Open Qualifier roster must participate in all starting lineups during subsequent stages of the tournament.

### 4.3 Roster Size & Locking

- **Roster Limit:** Total team members must not exceed 8 (5 players, 2 stand-ins, and 1 coach).
- **Single Roster Rule:** No player transfers are allowed within the tournament; a player participating for one team cannot play for another participating team.

### 4.4 VRS Core Roster Rule (3/5 Rule)

Teams accepting a VRS Invite or qualifying via Open Qualifiers must maintain at least three (3) out of five (5) players from their original core roster in every match. Failure to meet this condition results in replacement or disqualification.

### 4.5 Stand-in Rules

- Stand-ins are eligible only if they have not participated for another team in the tournament.
- Stand-ins must be approved by Tournament Officials at least 1 hour prior to the match start.
- Stand-ins cannot be added mid-map; roster substitutions are only allowed between matches or maps.

### 4.6 Coach Rules & Guidelines

- Teams may designate one individual as a coach.
- Coaches may join the team's official voice communication (TeamSpeak) but cannot connect to the game server as a spectator.
- Coaches are permitted to speak with players during Tactical Timeouts, Half Time & Freeze Time.
- Players may provide a private tactical stream to their coach; unauthorised distribution results in penalties or disqualification.

### 4.7 Team Assets & Naming Policy

- Team names must align with recognised public platforms (HLTV / Liquipedia).
- Names, nicknames, and logos must not contain offensive, racist, sexist, or discriminatory language.
- Sponsor tags in team names are prohibited unless pre-authorised.

### 4.8 Team Sponsors & Commercial Partners

- **Prohibited Sponsor Categories:** Team sponsors or partners that are deemed inappropriate, illegal, or offensive, or which could negatively impact the reputation and competitive integrity of Hotspawn, are strictly prohibited from being promoted during tournament participation. Prohibited categories include, but are not limited to:
    - Pornography, adult content, or sexually explicit material.
    - Illegal drugs, controlled substances, or drug paraphernalia.
    - Firearms, weapons, or ammunition.
    - Unregulated, illegal, or predatory financial products and services.
- **Skin Gambling, Skins Betting & Case-Opening Websites:** Sponsors associated with skin gambling, skins betting, or third-party case-opening websites are strictly prohibited from receiving broadcast or event exposure.
    - **Apparel & Jersey Coverings:** Any logos or branding representing skin gambling, skins betting, or case-opening sites on player jerseys or team apparel must be completely covered (e.g., using physical tape or patches) during all match days, broadcast streams, media days, and LAN Finals appearances.
    - **Digital & In-Game Branding:** Prohibited sponsor branding may not be included in player in-game names, broadcast overlays, or official team assets.
- **Compliance with Valve & Broadcast Standards:** All display of team sponsors must fully comply with applicable laws, broadcast platform Terms of Service (Twitch, YouTube, Kick, etc.), and Valve Corporation policies.
- **Admin Authority & Discretion:** Tournament Officials reserve the right to review team sponsors at any time and demand the immediate removal, masking, or concealment of non-compliant branding. All decisions rendered by Hotspawn administration regarding sponsor eligibility are final and non-negotiable.

### 5.2 Server Settings & Match Configurations

- **CSTV Delay:** 120 seconds.

### 5.3 Map Veto Procedures

> **Note:** On FACEIT, maps include a knife round. The team with map choice forfeits the knife round so the opposing team selects starting side.
> 

#### Best-of-One (BO1) Veto

- Team 1 (Left Side) chooses who bans first.
- Team A - Ban
- Team B - Ban
- Team B - Ban
- Team A - Ban
- Team A - Ban
- Team B - Ban
- Final Map left over played with side chosen by knife round.

#### Best-of-Three (BO3) Veto

- Team 1 (Left Side) chooses who bans first.
- Team A - Ban
- Team B - Ban
- Team A - Pick Map 1 (Team B chooses side)
- Team B - Pick Map 2 (Team A chooses side)
- Team A - Ban
- Team B - Ban
- Final Map left over as Decider (Knife round for sides).

### 5.4 Punctuality & Delays

#### 5.4.1 Open Qualifiers

- **Veto Readiness:** Team captains must be present and ready to initiate the map veto process at the officially scheduled match time.
- **Server Connect Time:** All ten (10) players must join the game server and be fully ready to play within ten (10) minutes following the completion of the map veto.
- **Unexcused Delays:** Any unexcused delay beyond the specified connect window will result in an automatic map forfeiture (recorded as a 13–0 loss).
- **Technical Exceptions & Matchroom Restarts:** If a team fails to ready up in the matchroom solely due to a technical issue, the matchroom may be restarted without penalty, provided the affected player is ready to play and actively communicating with admins within the matchroom.

---

#### 5.4.2 Online Group Stage

- **Official Schedule Compliance:** All official match start times will be confirmed and posted by Tournament Officials within the designated Discord server.
- **Veto Attendance Requirement:** The team captain (or authorised veto representative) must be present in the official Discord match channel and ready to start the map veto process exactly thirty (30) minutes prior to the scheduled match start time.
- **Veto Grace Period:** If a team representative is not ready at the 30-minute mark, a ten (10) minute grace period is applied (allowing appearance up to 20 minutes prior to scheduled match start time).
- **Server Readiness Window:** All five starting players per team must be connected to the game server and ready to begin play no later than ten (10) minutes prior to the scheduled match start time.
- **Inter-Map & Inter-Series Breaks:** A mandatory fifteen (15) minute break is enforced between maps within a series, as well as a minimum fifteen (15) minute buffer between separate series to absorb schedule variations, unless explicitly altered by Tournament Officials.
- **Veto Forfeiture & Randomisation Procedure:** Failure to appear or participate in the map veto process before the grace period expires results in an immediate forfeiture of all veto privileges. In such cases, Tournament Officials reserve the right to complete the veto process on behalf of the absent team utilising automated platform functions or neutral third-party randomisation tools (such as [random.org](http://random.org/)). Veto outcomes executed under this rule are final and non-negotiable.
- **Reporting Delays:** Teams must report any potential scheduling delays to Tournament Officials immediately via Discord. Hotspawn reserves full authority to grant or deny delay requests at its sole discretion without obligation to provide justification.

### 5.5 Pauses (Tactical & Technical)

#### Tactical Pauses

- Teams receive three (3) 30-second tactical timeouts per map.
- One (1) additional timeout is granted per overtime period.
- Initiated via menu or commands (`!timeout`, `.timeout`, `!tac`, `.tac`).

#### Technical Pauses

- Teams receive 5 minutes of technical pause time per map.
- Initiated via commands (`!pause`, `.pause`, `!tech`, `.tech`).
- Teams may donate technical pause time to opponents.
- Exhausting technical pause time without resolving issues forces the team to resume or forfeit.

### 5.6 Technical Interruptions, Disconnections & Restores

#### 5.6.1 Universal Principles & Player Conduct

- **Mandatory Completion:** Once a round becomes live, players MUST play the round to its full conclusion regardless of disconnections or technical disruptions, unless explicitly paused or stopped by a Tournament Official.
- **Prohibited Chat Invalidation:** Players are strictly forbidden from typing "NL", "Not Live", "off", or stopping movement in an attempt to void a live round. Doing so may result in losing the contested round or receiving minor warnings.
- **Round Restore Authority:** Round restores, replays, or server rollbacks are executed exclusively by Tournament Officials using official backup files.

---

#### 5.6.2 Stage-Specific Technical Rules

#### A. Open Qualifiers (FACEIT Platform)

- **Player-Initiated Technical Pause:** Teams must trigger technical pauses using the in-game command (`!tech` / `.tech` or the FACEIT matchroom interface) during freeze time.
- **5-Minute Limit:** Because matches are hosted on FACEIT, player-initiated technical pauses are strictly limited by the platform to **five (5) minutes per team per map**.
- **Disconnection Before Damage:** If a player drops or experiences a technical disruption before any damage is dealt or significant information is gained, the affected team must trigger a technical pause during freeze time to allow the player to reconnect.
- **Disconnection After Damage:** If a player drops after damage has been dealt, the ongoing round MUST be played to completion. The pause will activate during the next freeze time.
- **Pause Exhaustion:** If a player cannot reconnect within the allotted 5-minute player technical pause, the team must resume play 4v5 or forfeit the match.

#### B. Online Group Stage (FACEIT Admin-Monitored)

- **Player & Admin Pauses:** Matches will be hosted on FACEIT, where player-initiated technical pauses (`!tech` / `.tech`) remain capped at **five (5) minutes**. However, because Tournament Officials are assigned to match lobbies, admins possess the ability to execute an **Admin Pause** if additional time is required to resolve a technical issue or investigate an incident.
- **Replay & Restore Criteria:**
    - **Before First Kill / Damage:** If a player disconnects or experiences a critical client/server failure before the first kill or significant damage is dealt, an admin will execute a round restore to the start of that round.
    - **After First Kill / Damage:** If a failure occurs after the first kill or significant damage, the round will stand if the outcome can be clearly determined. If the outcome cannot be determined due to a server failure, the admin will restore the round.
- **Forfeiture & 4v5 Continuation Thresholds:** If an issue cannot be resolved via Admin Pause, a team may continue playing 4v5 **only if** one of the following conditions is met:
    1. The affected team has scored ten (10) or more rounds.
    2. The affected team holds a lead of six (6) or more rounds.
    3. Tournament Officials determine the team maintains a realistic competitive chance based on economy and game state.
    - *If none of these conditions are met, the map is awarded as a technical loss (13–0).*

#### C. LAN Finals (In-Person & Pracroom's)

- **Immediate Physical Alert:** Players must verbally and physically alert the in-room Tournament Admin immediately upon experiencing a technical or hardware issue.
- **Admin Pause Control:** All technical pauses at LAN are managed directly by Tournament Officials via admin commands.
- **Headsets & Silent Waiting:** During technical pauses, players not directly impacted by the issue must sit quietly with their headsets on. Talking, typing, or hand signals between players during a technical pause are strictly prohibited.
- **Equipment Buy Restoration:** If a round restore is ordered at LAN, both teams must purchase the exact same equipment owned prior to the restore.
- **Catastrophic Hardware Failures:** If local hardware (PC, monitor, network) fails completely and cannot be fixed within 15 minutes, local admins will facilitate an emergency swap to backup equipment.

---

#### 5.6.3 Early Map Restart Rule (All Stages)

A full map restart may be requested in limited emergency cases (e.g., critical Anti-Cheat or configuration errors upon joining), subject to admin approval, provided **ALL** of the following conditions are met:

1. No more than five (5) total rounds have been played.
2. The impacted team is the only team that has won any rounds in the map.
3. The request is submitted within the first 15 minutes of the technical issue / pause.
4. The replacement player or fix fully complies with all stand-in and VRS core roster requirements.
- *A team may only utilise an Early Map Restart once per series.*

### 7.4 Timeouts & Pauses

#### 7.4.1 Tactical Timeouts

- **Quota:** Teams receive up to **three (3) tactical timeouts** per map (30 seconds each).
- **Initiation:**
    - Players must initiate the pause in-game by typing `!timeout`.
    - A coach may initiate a timeout by physically tapping the team captain on the shoulder.
- **Rules During Tactical Pause:**
    - Coaches are permitted to communicate only while the timeout is active.
    - Timeouts may be stacked (all 3 used consecutively).
    - Players **must remain in their seats with headsets on** for the full duration.

### 7.4.2 Technical Timeouts & Round Restores

- **Reporting:** Teams must immediately alert the Tournament Admin in their room and specify the exact technical issue.
- **Conduct During Tech Pause:** Uninvolved players must wait quietly. Headsets **must stay on** unless instructed otherwise by an admin. All forms of player communication (typing, talking, hand signals) are strictly forbidden.
- **Round Restore Thresholds:**
    - **Before Damage / Under 60 Seconds:** If the technical issue occurs before any damage is dealt **OR** within the first 60 seconds of a round, the round will be restored to the start. Players must re-buy the **exact same equipment** owned prior to the restore.
    - **After Damage / Over 60 Seconds:** If damage was dealt or 60 seconds have elapsed, the round must be played out to completion. A technical pause will then be called during the subsequent freeze time.
- **Misused Pauses:** If a team calls a tactical pause to resolve a technical issue, that tactical pause will **not** be reinstated.

## 8.0 Code of Conduct & Penalties

### 8.1 Code of Conduct

Participants must exhibit sportsmanship and respect towards admins, opponents, and viewers. Toxic, racist, or harassing conduct leads to severe sanctions.

### 8.2 Penalty Hierarchy

1. **Verbal Warning:** Notice of rule infractions with no direct penalty.
